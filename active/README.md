# Xiang Kang Active

OEM and ODM activewear, fitness apparel, casualwear and custom team uniforms.

Static company website. Enquiries prepare an email draft in the visitor’s email application; no personal data is stored by a form backend.

Factory and customer sample photographs are supplied by Xiang Kang. Generated concept visuals are identified on the website. Named manufacturing and supply relationships are company-provided information. Third-party names and artwork remain with their owners. Customer designs are not offered for reproduction or resale.

## Catalogue
The collection edit contains sixteen original apparel design concepts with proposed colour palettes and development detail views. Product images are AI-generated illustrations; proposed fabric weights, construction and fit are not approved production specifications. Enquiries carry the selected style into an editable brief.
