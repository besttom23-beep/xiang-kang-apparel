MATERIALS & DETAILS

Built around
the product.

Premium cotton, Spandex, Polyester, nylon and lyocell are among the material options we can discuss for your collection. Yarn selection, fabric weight, blend composition and knit construction are developed around your garment, season, target price and destination market. Final specifications and any testing requirements are confirmed for each project.

Decoration options include screen printing, digital printing and embroidery.

A few useful starting points

Can we start with one sample?

Yes. Sample development can begin from one piece, subject to the product brief and sample cost.

What is the bulk minimum?

The typical minimum is 300 units per colour, distributed across multiple sizes.

How long does development take?

Sampling typically takes 2–3 weeks and bulk production 3–6 weeks. Timing depends on complexity, materials, quantity and final sample approval.

Can you help with freight?

Export documentation, customs and air or sea freight can be coordinated according to agreed commercial terms.
